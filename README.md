I am a **Principal-level AI/ML engineer and semiconductor domain expert** with **16+ years of post-silicon validation and product-engineering experience**. My work focuses on structured ML, wafer and yield analytics, test-time optimisation, root-cause analysis, and engineering automation.

I build practical predictive and agentic solutions with strong attention to data quality, evaluation, safety controls, and engineering decision-making. I also mentor engineers and interns, design structured learning paths and hands-on modules, and help domain experts apply Python, ML, GenAI, and MLOps to day-to-day engineering problems.

## Architecture method

<div align="center">
  <img src="assets/architecture-method.png" alt="Architecture workflow from a semiconductor engineering problem through data and provenance, model and agent orchestration, evaluation and safety gates, APIs and workflow integration, and engineer review with continuous feedback" width="100%">
</div>

- **Evidence:** deterministic evaluation, immutable artifacts, data and model hashes, uncertainty, and explicit evidence classes.
- **Quality:** leakage controls, class-level errors, calibration, shift sensitivity, and preserved negative results.
- **Safety:** confidence routing, bounded-risk policies, human review, drift gates, canary promotion, and rollback.
- **Delivery:** APIs, containers, CI/CD, data platforms, retrieval, knowledge graphs, and governed engineering workflows.
